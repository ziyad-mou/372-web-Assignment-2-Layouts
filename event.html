Campus Music Night

Category: Music

September 25, 2026 at 6:00 PM

Student Union Lawn

Campus Music Night brings students together for an evening of live music, food, and community activities. Student musicians and local performers will perform throughout the evening. Students are welcome to attend, relax, and enjoy the event with friends.

About the Event

Campus Music Night is designed to give students a fun way to connect with the campus community while supporting student performers.

The event will include several live performances and opportunities for students to meet campus organizations.

What to Expect

  • Live performances from student musicians.
  • Food and refreshments available nearby.
  • Outdoor seating areas for students and guests.
  • Information from participating student organizations.

Event Schedule

  1. 6:00 PM - Welcome and opening remarks
  2. 6:15 PM - First student performance
  3. 7:00 PM - Second student performance
  4. 7:45 PM - Local artist performance
  5. 8:30 PM - Closing performance

Accessibility and Participation

The event area includes accessible paths and seating. Students who need additional accommodations may contact the Office of Student Engagement before the event.
